# SOP: Property Viewing — Real Estate Agent

## Before the viewing

1. Confirm appointment 2 hours before via SMS or WhatsApp.
2. Pull client record from CRM: name, budget, preferences, last 3 viewings.
3. Print one-page property summary: address, price, area (m²), key features.

## During the viewing

- Greet client by name and confirm timing.
- Walk through rooms in this order: living room, kitchen, bedrooms, bathroom, balcony.
- Highlight 3 key selling points. Avoid overwhelming with details.
- Note client reactions: positive, neutral, concerned. Write down specific quotes.
- Ask open question: *"What's your first impression?"*
- Ask blocker question: *"What would stop you from making an offer today?"*

## After the viewing

- Within 2 hours: send thank-you SMS + property recap PDF.
- Within 24 hours: schedule follow-up call.
- Within 48 hours: if client is interested, send 2-3 comparable properties.
- If client is not interested: ask one open-ended *"what was missing?"* question for future matching.

## Key vocabulary

- **Cena za m²**: critical number, always know it.
- **Stan techniczny**: condition — be honest, don't oversell.
- **Wspólnota mieszkaniowa** vs **spółdzielnia**: matters for legal status and resale.
- **Hipoteka**: ask if client is cash buyer or financed — affects timeline.
