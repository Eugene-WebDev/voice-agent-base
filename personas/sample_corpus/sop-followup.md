# SOP: Client Follow-up

## Cadence

- Day 0 (viewing day): SMS thank-you + recap PDF.
- Day 1: phone call within business hours. If no answer, voicemail + WhatsApp.
- Day 3: if no response yet, send 2-3 comparable properties via email.
- Day 7: final check-in. If no response, mark client as cold in CRM and stop.

## Phone call structure

1. Start with name and warm opener: *"Cześć [name], tu [agent]. Jak Ci minął weekend?"*
2. Transition: *"Dzwonię w sprawie mieszkania na [adres] — masz minutę?"*
3. Listen. Don't push.
4. Surface objections: *"Co Cię w tym mieszkaniu zatrzymuje?"*
5. Address objections specifically, not generally.
6. Close with next step: either schedule second viewing, send comparables, or politely close the conversation.

## What NOT to do

- Don't call before 10am or after 8pm.
- Don't send more than 2 messages without a response.
- Don't pitch other properties on the first follow-up call — that's day 3+ territory.
- Don't ask leading questions (*"Don't you love the location?"*). Ask open ones.

## Escalation

If client misses 3 consecutive scheduled callbacks, send one final email:

> *"Wycofuję się — jeśli wrócisz w sprawie, jestem dostępny. Pozdrawiam."*

Then mark cold. Do not chase further.
